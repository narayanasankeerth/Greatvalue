**Interactive Multi-Dimensional Analytics Tool for OEM Benchmarking**

[🚀 View Live Project Dashboard](https://nakkalapallisureshbabu.github.io/Information-Visualization/)

## 📈 Executive Summary
This project delivers a high-fidelity prototype for automotive data visualization, designed to transform raw vehicle data (`a1-cars.csv`) into actionable business intelligence. By integrating coordinated visualizations, the system allows stakeholders to analyze key performance drivers—such as MPG, Horsepower, and Acceleration—across different manufacturing origins and model years.

## 🎯 Business Analytical Tasks
I identified and implemented four core visualization tasks to address typical business questions in the automotive sector:

1.  **Market Efficiency Benchmarking (Bubble Chart):** 
    *   *Task:* Compare fuel efficiency (MPG) vs. performance (Acceleration) scaled by Horsepower.
    *   *Insight:* Identifying high-performance, fuel-efficient outliers across American, Japanese, and European origins.
2.  **Product Configuration Distribution (Histogram):** 
    *   *Task:* Analyze the frequency of engine configurations (Cylinders).
    *   *Insight:* Understanding production volume trends and market saturation of specific engine types.
3.  **Historical Trend Analysis (Scatter Plot):** 
    *   *Task:* Track the evolution of MPG, Cylinders, and Acceleration over time (Model Year).
    *   *Insight:* Visualizing the industry shift toward higher MPG and fewer cylinders in newer models.
4.  **Competitive Origin Mapping (Heat Map):** 
    *   *Task:* Correlate specific manufacturers (e.g., VW, Volvo, BMW) with MPG and Model Year.
    *   *Insight:* Identifying brand-specific market niches and manufacturing focus shifts.
